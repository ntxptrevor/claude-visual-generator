## What this notice covers

NTXP provides company accounts and systems for company business. NTXP may monitor and review their use. This applies to:

- **Email:** company Google Workspace mail (@ntxpllc.com).
- **Calendar:** company Google Calendar, including meeting attendance.
- **Files:** company Google Drive, Docs and Sheets, including who opened or shared a file.
- **Project systems:** JobTread (daily logs, time, purchase orders, invoices) and QuickBooks.
- **Phone system:** the company phone system (call and text records, and recordings where permitted).
- **Meeting recordings:** company recording devices and their transcripts, where used.

Personal accounts and personal devices are not covered, except for company accounts accessed on them.

## Why NTXP monitors

- **Operations:** keep customers answered on time and projects on schedule.
- **Quality:** check that daily reports, records and billing are complete and accurate.
- **Security:** protect company and customer information and detect misuse.

## How the information is used

- **Automated tools** summarise activity (for example response times or missing report items) and may raise a **proposal** for the owners to review.
- **People decide.** A proposal is not a finding. The owners review the facts and context first. **No disciplinary action is ever taken automatically.**
- **Emotional-tone indicators** may be generated for messages **between NTXP employees only**. They are **internal**, seen only by the owners, and are never shared with customers, vendors or other employees. They are never generated for messages with people outside NTXP.
- **Access** to monitoring results is limited to the owners.
- **No expectation of privacy** applies to company accounts and systems. Do not use them for private matters you want to keep private.
